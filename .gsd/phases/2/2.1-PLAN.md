# Plan 2.1: Favorites Playback Reconstruction

## Objective
Enable playback of video items directly from the Favorites screen. A `Favorite` entry only saves the source and media ID, not the transient `MediaItem` (which includes expiring URLs and tokens). We must add functionality to resynthesize the `MediaItem` on demand.

## Context
- .gsd/SPEC.md
- app/src/main/java/com/pscholer/autoplayer/data/remote/jellyfin/JellyfinApi.kt
- app/src/main/java/com/pscholer/autoplayer/data/remote/plex/PlexApi.kt
- app/src/main/java/com/pscholer/autoplayer/data/local/LocalMediaRepository.kt
- app/src/main/java/com/pscholer/autoplayer/car/screens/FavoritesScreen.kt
